Top 5 Converting Verticals in 2025

Published on September 26, 2025

The affiliate marketing landscape is always evolving. Staying ahead of the curve means knowing which verticals are hot right now. Based on our network's performance data, here are the top 5 verticals you should be focusing on in 2025.

1. iGaming & Sports Betting

With ongoing legalization in new markets, iGaming is exploding. This vertical includes online casinos, poker, and sports betting. It boasts some of the highest CPA and RevShare payouts in the industry. Look for major sporting events to drive seasonal traffic.

2. Health & Wellness (Nutra)

The Nutra vertical remains an evergreen powerhouse. Offers for weight loss, supplements, skincare, and fitness programs consistently convert well. This niche is driven by strong consumer demand for self-improvement, making it highly resilient.

3. Finance & Insurance Tech

As more people manage their finances online, the demand for financial products and services has soared. This includes lead generation for loans, credit cards, insurance, and investment apps. These CPL (Cost Per Lead) offers can be extremely lucrative.

4. E-commerce & Shopping

The shift to online shopping is permanent. While competitive, there are endless sub-niches to explore, from fashion and electronics to home goods and pet supplies. Conversion rates are high, especially around holidays and major sales events.

5. Mobile Content & Apps

The app market is bigger than ever. CPI (Cost Per Install) offers for mobile games, utilities, and entertainment apps are a great way to monetize mobile traffic. Subscription-based services within this vertical also provide a steady stream of recurring revenue.
