A Guide to Understanding Affiliate Marketing Payout Models

Published on September 25, 2025

Understanding the different ways you can get paid is fundamental to your success as an affiliate marketer. The payout model determines what action a user needs to take for you to earn a commission. Let's break down the most common models.

CPA (Cost Per Action/Acquisition)

CPA is one of the most popular models. You get paid when a user completes a specific action, which could be making a purchase, signing up for a newsletter, or filling out a form. It's a versatile model that advertisers love because they only pay for results.

CPL (Cost Per Lead)

A subset of CPA, CPL focuses specifically on lead generation. You earn a commission when a user provides their contact information by filling out a form. This is common in the finance, insurance, and home services verticals.

CPS (Cost Per Sale)

Also known as PPS (Pay Per Sale), this is a straightforward model common in e-commerce. You earn a commission, usually a percentage of the sale amount, when a user you refer makes a purchase. Your payout is directly tied to the revenue you generate.

RevShare (Revenue Share)

With RevShare, you earn a percentage of the revenue generated by the customers you refer for their entire lifetime. This model is common in subscription services and the iGaming vertical. While it may take longer to earn, it offers the potential for high, long-term passive income from a single conversion.

"Choosing the right payout model is about matching your traffic's intent with the offer's requirements. There is no single 'best' model—only what's best for your audience."
